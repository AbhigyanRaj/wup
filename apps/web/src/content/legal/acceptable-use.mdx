This policy lists what you may not do with wuup. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms). It exists to protect you, other users, the people whose data passes through wuup, and the systems wuup connects to. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask us before you do it.

## Prohibited uses

Do not use wuup to:

- break any law, or help anyone else break one;
- process personal data without a lawful basis, or in breach of the DPDP Act or other data protection laws that apply to you;
- harass, discriminate against, or make decisions about people that have legal or similarly significant effects (such as credit, employment or housing decisions) based solely on wuup's output;
- create or spread malware, spam, phishing or fraudulent content;
- infringe intellectual property or other rights;
- generate content that sexualises minors, promotes violence or terrorism, or otherwise breaks the usage policies of the AI provider answering your question.

## Data you must not connect

Only connect or upload data you have the right to use. In particular, do not connect:

- databases, sheets or documents that belong to someone else without their permission;
- credentials you are not authorised to use, or a database user with write or admin rights when a `read`-only user would do;
- payment card data, government identity numbers, health records, biometric data or passwords in plain text, unless they are excluded from the bridge scope or hidden as fields. See [Scopes and redaction](/docs/scopes-and-redaction).

wuup hides fields that look sensitive automatically, but you are responsible for reviewing the hidden-field list for your data.

## Security and abuse

Do not:

- probe, scan or test the vulnerability of wuup, or try to get around its read-only guard, scopes, hidden fields, query limits or rate limits;
- use wuup to reach systems you are not allowed to access, including private networks and other people's databases;
- access another user's account or data;
- interfere with the Service, overload it, or send automated traffic beyond normal use of the app;
- resell or share access to your account, or use the free tier through multiple accounts to avoid its limits.

wuup applies rate limits to sign-in, uploads, demo sessions and chat, and may slow down or block traffic that looks automated or abusive.

## Responsible disclosure

If you find a security issue, report it privately to [abhigyan23018@iiitd.ac.in](mailto:abhigyan23018@iiitd.ac.in) with steps to reproduce. Test only against your own account and data, do not access other users' data, and give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it. We will not pursue good-faith research that follows these rules.

## Enforcement

If you break this policy we may remove content, disable a connection, limit features, or suspend or close your account, depending on how serious the breach is. Where reasonable we will tell you what happened and how to fix it. We may report illegal activity to the authorities.

## Reporting a problem

To report abuse, a policy breach or content that should not be on wuup, email [abhigyan23018@iiitd.ac.in](mailto:abhigyan23018@iiitd.ac.in) with as much detail as you can. For privacy requests, see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
